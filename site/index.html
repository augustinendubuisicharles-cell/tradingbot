How the rule works

Eight checks per coin look at whether price is making new highs over 10 to 360 days. Each check that's "up" adds to the position, and each has a trailing stop that only rises. Positions shrink when a coin is very jumpy, so a crash costs less. Decided on the daily close, traded at the next open, fees included.

It makes less than holding in strong bull markets (2023-24) and avoids most of the big crashes (2018, 2022). From published research (Zarattini et al. 2025), with their settings, not tuned to our data.
